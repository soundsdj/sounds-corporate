A few helpful details

Before you get in touch.

What is included in both packages?

Both packages include planning, sound equipment, basic dance floor lighting, and wireless microphones. The Corporate Public Event includes up to 5 hours of DJ coverage; the Corporate Holiday Party includes up to 7 hours.

Are MC services included?

MC services are an optional add-on. Let me know what hosting or announcements you need when you inquire.

Can the music be tailored to our guests?

Yes. We’ll discuss your audience and the atmosphere you want during planning, and I’ll read the room and adjust the music during the event.

Can we add lighting or a photo booth?

Yes. Architectural uplighting and a photo booth are available as optional add-ons.

What if we need more time?

Additional time is available at an extra cost. Include your expected schedule in your inquiry so we can discuss the coverage you need.

How do we get started?

Share your event details through the corporate inquiry page. Tell me what you’re planning, where it’s happening, and the date you have in mind.
